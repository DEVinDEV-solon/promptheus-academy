# Memex

**Type:** Concept  
**Tags:** History, Knowledge Systems, Bush, Visionary  
**Zuletzt aktualisiert:** 2026-04-06  
**Quellen:** *s02_llm_wiki*, *e04_bush*

## Zusammenfassung

**Memex = "Memory Extension"** — Vannevar Bush's 1945 visionary concept für eine persönliche, Kuratierte Wissensdatenbank mit **assoziativen Pfaden** zwischen Dokumenten. Der geistige Vorläufer zu modernen *Knowledge Base Designs*.

## Bush's Vision (1945)

Nach dem WWII: Zu viel veröffentlichte Forschung, Menschliche kann nicht mithalten.

**Solution: Memex** — Ein Desktop-Apparat mit:
- Personal curated document collection
- **Associative trails** (Links!) zwischen Dokumenten
- User can create custom connections
- Records user's research process
- Private, personal, non-hierarchical

## Memex vs Klassische Bibliotheken

❌ **Hierarchical:** Dewey Decimal, strict categories
✓ **Memex:** Associative, user-defined connections

## The Memex Problem (Unsolved in 1945)

**Who maintains the associations?**
- Sehr tedious
- Humans abandon it
- Maintenance burden > perceived value

## How Web Deviated from Memex

Bush imagined: Curated, private, associative, maintained
Web became: Public, algorithmic, search-ranked, scattered

**Quote from *s02_llm_wiki*:**
> "Bush's vision was closer to this than to what the web became: private, actively curated, with the connections between documents as valuable as the documents themselves. The part he couldn't solve was who does the maintenance. The LLM handles that."

## Modern Reincarnation

*LLM Wiki Pattern* = **Memex + LLM Maintenance**

- Personal curated sources (like Memex)
- Persistent wiki with associative links (like Memex)
- **LLM maintains cost→0** (solving Bush's problem!)

## Timeline

- 1945: Bush describes Memex (hypothetical)
- 1960s-1990s: Hypertext research inspired by Memex (Nelson, Engelbart)
- 1990s-2000s: WWW created (doesn't match Bush's vision)
- 2020s: Personal wiki tools (Roam, Obsidian, etc.)
- 2025+: *LLM Wiki* (Memex finally practical!)
