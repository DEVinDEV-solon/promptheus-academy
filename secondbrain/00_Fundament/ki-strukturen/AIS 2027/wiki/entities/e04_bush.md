## Zusammenfassung

Vannevar Bush (1890-1974), Ingenieur und Wissenschaftler, der 1945 das **Memex-Konzept** vorschlug. Historischer Vorläufer zu modernen [[10 Wissen/AIS 2027/wiki/concepts/c05_kb_design]] und LLM Wiki Systemen.

## Das Memex Konzept (1945)

Essay "As We May Think" — hypothetische Maschine:
- Personal, desktop-sized knowledge store
- Curated collection of documents
- **Associative trails** zwischen Dokumenten (Links!)
- User can create custom connections

## Memex vs Web

✓ Memex: Personal, private, curated, associative trails, maintained over time
✗ Web: Public, search-ranked, scattered, passive consumption

**Quote:** "The part he couldn't solve was who does the maintenance. The LLM handles that."

## The Missing Piece: Maintenance

1945: Human curator does it full-time (impractical)
2026: LLM Agent does it (near-zero cost)
